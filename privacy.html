Privacy policy

the-system is used only by its owner, with his own Google accounts.

  • It reads Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive data from those accounts. It can edit only files it created itself.
  • The data is kept on the owner's computer and processed with Anthropic's Claude to answer the owner's own requests.
  • It is never sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
